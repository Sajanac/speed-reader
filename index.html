How to install

Mac (Safari): show the Favorites bar (View › Show Favorites Bar), then drag the Speed Read button above onto it. With iCloud Safari sync on, it also appears on your iPhone and iPad.

iPhone / iPad: tap Copy bookmarklet code. Bookmark any page (Share › Add Bookmark, name it “Speed Read”). Then open Bookmarks, tap Edit, select the new bookmark, and replace its address with the copied code.

Using it: open an article in Safari, open Bookmarks, and tap Speed Read. The reader opens in a new tab with the article loaded.

    Voice

    No sound on iPhone? Check the volume, and use the reader in a Safari tab (speech is unreliable in Home Screen apps). This page asks iOS to play sound even when the silent switch is on; if you still hear nothing, flip the switch off and try again.

    Voice speed and one-word reading speed are separate. For better device voices, download Enhanced or Premium voices in your device's Accessibility settings.

    Keyboard

    One-word mode keeps the page quiet while playing; tap to pause. Read-along mode keeps the text and controls available.

    Space: play/pause · ←/→: back/forward 10 words · ↑/↓: speed
